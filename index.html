— Substance —

The Spice Melange

The most valuable substance in the universe. Found only on Arrakis, the spice extends life, expands consciousness, and is vital to interstellar travel.

⏳

Life Extension

Regular consumption of the spice can extend human lifespan by centuries and enhance vitality.

👁

Prescience

The spice grants limited prescience — the ability to see possible futures — a gift and a burden.

🚀

Space Navigation

Navigator Guild Heighliners rely on spice-driven prescience to fold space and travel between stars.

⚠

Warning: Spice withdrawal is fatal. The substance creates total physical and psychological dependence. Control the spice, control the universe.

— The Great Houses —

Noble Families

The Imperium is a web of political intrigue, feudal obligation, and dynastic ambition.

🦅

House Atreides

"Here I am, here I remain."

Ruled by Duke Leto Atreides, House Atreides is known for honor, justice, and loyalty among the Great Houses. Their fief on the ocean planet Caladan is a paradise of rain and greenery — a stark contrast to Arrakis, where the Emperor has assigned them the dangerous task of spice harvesting.

  • Seat: Caladan (formerly)
  • Leader: Duke Leto Atreides I
  • Sigil: Red hawk on green field
  • Trait: Honor and integrity
🐏

House Harkonnen

"Fear is the mind-killer."

The Harkonnen dynasty, led by the grotesquely obese Baron Vladimir Harkonnen, thrives on cruelty, exploitation, and treachery. Their homeworld of Giedi Prime is an industrial wasteland of pollution and slave labor. For generations, they have been the bitter enemies of House Atreides.

  • Seat: Giedi Prime
  • Leader: Baron Vladimir Harkonnen
  • Sigil: Black ram on yellow field
  • Trait: Cruelty and greed
🦁

House Corrino

"The throne is earned by blood."

The Corrino family has ruled the known universe for over 10,000 years since the Butlerian Jihad. Padishah Emperor Shaddam IV sits on the Golden Lion Throne, but his power is balanced — and threatened — by the Landsraad of Great Houses and the Spacing Guild.

  • Seat: Kaitain
  • Leader: Padishah Emperor Shaddam IV
  • Sigil: Golden lion
  • Trait: Political cunning
— The Desert People —

The Fremen

The native people of Arrakis. Freedmen who have survived the desert for millennia, the Fremen are fierce warriors, expert water conservationists, and deeply spiritual people who await the coming of the Lisan al-Gaib — the voice from the outer world who will lead them to paradise.

The Prophecy

The Assignment

Emperor Shaddam IV transfers control of Arrakis from House Harkonnen to House Atreides — a trap disguised as an honor.

The Betrayal

The Sardaukar, disguised as Harkonnen troops, storm Arrakeen. Duke Leto is betrayed by his own physician, Dr. Yueh, and killed.

The Desert

Paul Atreides and Lady Jessica flee into the deep desert, where they encounter the Fremen of Sietch Tabr. Paul must prove himself among the desert's harshest warriors.

The Emergence

Paul Atreides becomes Muad'Dib, unites the Fremen, and leads a revolution that will shake the foundations of the known universe. The spice must flow.
